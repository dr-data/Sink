## 2024-05-15 - [KV Eventual Consistency & Inline Migrations]
**Learning:** In Cloudflare Workers KV, inline migrations (e.g., reading an item without metadata, then writing it back with metadata during a search operation) can trigger massive write operation spikes if the dataset is large or the endpoint is hit frequently. Due to eventual consistency, the `KV.put` may not propagate immediately, causing subsequent calls to see the old version and trigger another `KV.put`. This also blocks the main request response, leading to severe performance degradation.
**Action:** Avoid doing synchronous state migrations (like KV.put) inside high-frequency read endpoints like `/search` or `/list`. Wait to perform migrations during explicit update actions, or handle them via background processes (like Cron Triggers or Queue workers) to keep reads fast and write operations low.
